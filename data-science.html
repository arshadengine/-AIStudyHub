Data Science Notes & Resources

Master the end-to-end data science lifecycle: from data acquisition, cleaning, and exploratory analysis to statistical modeling and storytelling.

Exploratory Data Analysis Data Cleaning & Imputation Data Visualization Statistical Inference View DS Syllabus →
Step 01

Data Cleaning & Imputation

Handling missing values (mean, median, KNN imputer), removing duplicates, outlier detection using IQR and Z-scores, and managing data inconsistencies.

Step 02

Exploratory Data Analysis

Univariate and bivariate analysis, summary statistics, correlation matrices (Pearson & Spearman), distribution skewness, and feature interaction checks.

Step 03

Data Visualization

Designing insightful visual graphics using Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly: distribution histograms, boxplots, pairplots, and heatmaps.

Step 04

Inferential Statistics

Hypothesis testing (t-tests, ANOVA, Chi-Square), Central Limit Theorem, confidence intervals, p-value interpretation, and A/B testing principles.

Step 05

Feature Engineering

One-hot encoding for nominal variables, ordinal encoding, log transformations for skewed features, and normalization vs. standardization.
